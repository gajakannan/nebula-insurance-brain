`corpus.yaml`, `CORPUS-HASHES.txt` and `README.md` are generated by `build_corpus.py`. The generator also checks its own spec:
- Each tag is consistent with the evidence. `unique` means exactly 1 evidence entry. `repeated` means 2 or more. `table-borne` means every evidence region is a table cell. A `split-across-regions` value has 2 parts on the same page, and joining them with one space gives the value.
- Each non-split value is printed exactly as many times as it has evidence entries, so no stray copies exist.
- A distractor never equals a target value in the same document.
- For an `absent` field, the field's label text (for example "each occurrence", "named insured" or "policy period") is not printed anywhere in that document.
- No evidence is on a damaged page.
- Every field × tag cell in the coverage matrix is non-empty.
- The corpus has 6 native, 4 scanned, 2 mixed and 1 partial document.

## Limitations and notes

- **Split dollar amounts.** gl-q-004 prints the currency sign and the amount in adjacent table cells (`$` | `500,000`). Under the joining rule, the expected values are `$ 500,000` and `$ 1,000,000`, with a space after the sign. The comparator in the scoring step needs to know about this convention.
- **Partial document is a 13th document.** The damaged-page document is gl-q-013, kind `partial`. I added it so the 6 native, 4 scanned and 2 mixed counts stay exact.
- **What the damage does.** The damage is a broken filter chain (ASCII85 then Flate). pypdf raises an error on it. I did not run Docling, so I have not observed how Docling behaves on this page; the check covers pypdf only. pypdfium2 rendered the page without raising an error, which suggests it renders as blank or partial.
- **Absent fields.** An absent field is omitted entirely, including its label. The document does not say "see schedule". gl-q-005 explains a missing expiration date with "Policy Term: Continuous until cancelled".
- **Synthetic data.** All entities, addresses (fictional towns, ZIP codes ending in 999), policy numbers and form numbers are invented. No document value contains the word "Synthetic".
- **Scope.** The corpus is small: 13 documents and 65 field expectations. It is meant for development qualification, not for statistical acceptance.
