At 300 s select failure immediately, disable this attempt's publication capability, and record `conversion_timeout` with the deadline/outcome in the job's fenced failure path. Do not wait for child cleanup to choose the outcome. Send SIGTERM to the **whole child process group**, allow at most 2 s for exit, then SIGKILL and bounded reap (at most 3 s). Check that parser/OCR helper descendants are gone, not just that `waitpid` returned for the group leader; preflight rejects a pinned converter/helper that escapes this group. These ≤5 s are cleanup overhead after a failed conversion; they never extend the 300-second decision or permit late ready/model activity. Cancellation, authority/fence loss and parent exceptions use the same revoked-publication/cleanup path with their own reason. If the group cannot be confirmed gone, stop the worker, retain the guard and private staging for explicit DevOps recovery; do not start another conversion or release its ownership as if clean.
