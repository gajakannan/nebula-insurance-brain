# Cedar Bridge on foundation IRIs (F0012-S0003 AC7)

The account-scoped Cedar Bridge cases re-expressed on the production foundation release
(`ontology/releases/foundation/1`, term IRIs `urn:nebula:ont:foundation.*`):

- **EX-PC-001:** account and organization identity (`queries/account-status.rq`).
- **EX-PC-011:** roles are data and grant nothing.
- **EX-PC-015:** ownership versus the designated parent (`queries/ownership.rq`).
- **EX-PC-017:** account-scoped rows (`queries/account-team.rq`).
- **EX-PC-021:** close and reopen (`boundaries/account-close-reopen.ttl`).

The policy-dependent cases EX-PC-016/018/019/020 stay on the illustrative `pc:` fixtures until F0013.

`snapshot.ttl`, `snapshot.jsonld`, `shapes.ttl`, `queries/*` and `boundaries/*` are generated by
`scripts/validation/build_foundation_examples.py` from the `pc:` fixtures. `expected-results.yaml` and
`untyped-domain-probe.ttl` are authored. `scripts/validation/validate_semantic_rdf.py --require` checks:

- isomorphism of the release and the snapshot;
- the expected subclass entailments and the forbidden domain/range typing;
- every SHACL case (the data graph is the case plus `release.ttl`);
- exact and forbidden query rows.

`scripts/validation/validate_semantic_examples.py` checks that every `urn:nebula:ont:` IRI used here
exists in the compiled release.
