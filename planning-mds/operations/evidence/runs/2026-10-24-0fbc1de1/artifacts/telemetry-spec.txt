292-## Verdicts & Recommendations
293-
294-Canonical **passing** verdicts: `PASS`, `PASS WITH RECOMMENDATIONS`,
295-`APPROVED`, `APPROVED WITH RECOMMENDATIONS`. Canonical **blocking**
296-verdicts: `FAIL`, `REQUEST CHANGES`, `REJECTED`. `NOT_REQUIRED` is valid
297-only for non-required roles/gates.
298-
299-Recommendations under a `WITH RECOMMENDATIONS` verdict must each be a
300-discrete, parseable item in the role report:
301-
302-```
303-- [severity] <text> — owner: <name-or-role>; follow-up: <ticket | deferred-to-story | explicit-deferred-no-followup>
304-```
305-
306-Severity is exactly one of `low` / `medium` / `high` / `critical`. `low`
307-and `medium` need disposition (owner + follow-up). `high` and `critical`
308-are **blocking** — they cannot be closeout-passing unless `pm-closeout.md`
309-explicitly mitigates them via the **PM Acceptance Line**:
310-
311-```
312-- Accepted: <identifier> — <type-specific details>
313-```
314-
315-The PM Acceptance Line is the one canonical form for *every* PM acceptance
316-(coverage waiver → identifier `coverage`; validator-defect waiver →
317-the rule ID; unknown waiver key → the key; high/critical recommendation →
318-recommendation ID/text with a `mitigation:` prefix). The validator accepts
319-`—` or `-` as the separator and is case-insensitive on `Accepted:` and
320-`mitigation:`.
