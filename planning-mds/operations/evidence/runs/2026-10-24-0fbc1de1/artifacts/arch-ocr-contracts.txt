planning-mds/features/archive/F0004-content-artifact-model/feature-assembly-plan.md-167-9. **Integrity sweep.** `brain-content-integrity-sweep` is a new operator CLI (`neuron/packages/brain-ingestion/src/brain_ingestion/integrity_sweep.py`, DevOps runbook). It is synchronous, so it authorizes the way Step 2 item 2 does, never through the async `AuthorizationExecution` and never through the worker's separate pre-step gate (`document_delivery.py`, which commits its decision before the job step).
