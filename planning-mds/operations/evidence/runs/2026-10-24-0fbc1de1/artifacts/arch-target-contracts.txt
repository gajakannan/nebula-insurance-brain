1: ## Story Header
2: 
3: **Story ID:** F0005-S0004
4: 
5: **Feature:** F0005 — One-time document ingestion
6: 
7: **Title:** Evaluate and activate the proven pipeline
8: 
9: **Priority:** High
10: 
11: **Phase:** MVP — v0.1A
12: 
13: **Status:** In Progress
14: 
15: ## User Story
16: 
17: **As a** platform owner
18: 
19: **I want** a measured direct-versus-Graph comparison against agreed thresholds, followed by activation or rejection
20: 
21: **So that** Graph replaces the direct client only on evidence, and either outcome is recorded and reversible.
22: 
23: ## Context & Background
24: 
25: Implements the [PRD](PRD.md) under proposed [ADR-0060](../../architecture/decisions/ADR-0060-docling-graph-document-pipeline-orchestration.md). Delivered 2026-09-22: a recorded-response [synthetic comparison](synthetic-comparison.json), which is orchestration proof, not model quality. This plan adds the live corpus comparison, reviewer verdicts and the activation decision.
26: 
27: ## Acceptance Criteria
28: 
29: - [ ] **F0005-S0004-AC1:** Given the qualification corpus (at least 12 synthetic documents: 6 native, 4 scanned, 2 mixed-page), when it is committed, then every expected value and evidence location was authored from the document source by a role that does not run or review the extraction, and the corpus and expectation hashes are recorded before the first measured run.
30: - [ ] **F0005-S0004-AC2:** Given the direct baseline and Graph dense under recorded configurations (pins, model and tokenizer revision, chunking, budgets), when both profiles run over the full corpus, then the report records per-case and aggregate schema validity, field accuracy, abstention, fabricated evidence, evidence resolution, calls, serving-tokenizer tokens, p95 latency and review items per document.
31: - [ ] **F0005-S0004-AC3:** Given the report, when it is compared with the PRD thresholds, then each threshold is marked met or missed with its measured value; a missed threshold blocks activation and is never re-run with relaxed budgets to pass.
32: - [ ] **F0005-S0004-AC4:** Given S0001–S0003 and S0005 evidence plus the report, when QA, Architect and Security review them, then each records a verdict with evidence references in STATUS, and the operator records acceptance or rejection.
33: - [ ] **F0005-S0004-AC5:** Given all thresholds met, every verdict passing and operator acceptance, when activation runs, then ADR-0060 becomes Accepted, the worker default extraction contract becomes Graph dense through committed non-secret configuration, the v2 writer is enabled per S0005, and the dependency matrix, build/run metadata and C4 runtime description are updated together.
34: - [ ] **F0005-S0004-AC6:** Given an activated configuration, when the rollback setting selects the direct baseline, then new runs use the direct client with its own identity, existing artifacts and runs stay readable and unchanged, and no run mixes engines.
35: - [ ] **F0005-S0004-AC7:** Given any threshold miss, failing verdict or operator rejection, when the feature closes, then the direct client stays default, the v2 writer stays disabled, ADR-0060 records the failed gates (Proposed or Rejected per operator), and no partial activation remains.
36: - [ ] **F0005-S0004-AC8:** Given a job, artifact, run or activation state change, when the operation succeeds or fails, then an audit event records actor and job identity, correlation and attempt IDs, artifact/run references, outcome and timestamp, without secrets or source text.
37: 
38: **Edge Cases & Error Scenarios:** Live model unavailable during measurement → the run is void and repeated in full, never partially merged. Baseline worse than Graph on every metric → still reported; the baseline remains the rollback path. Reviewer finds an expectation error → the corrected expectation is re-hashed and both engines are re-measured.

47: | Committed configuration | Activate or roll back | Default extraction contract and `content.v2_writer_enabled` | Next job uses the selected engine and writer | Restart worker; submit a document; inspect run and manifest identity | Activation only after AC4 acceptance |

58:

62: 
63: ## Dependencies

65: S0001–S0003, S0005; agent-role QA, Architect and Security reviewers; operator acceptance; F0026 reuses the cases.
66: 
67: ## Out of Scope

69: Production host activation, licensed or frozen-holdout corpora, retiring the direct client code (it stays as the rollback path).
70: 
71: ## Questions & Assumptions

1: ## Story Header
2: 
3: **Story ID:** F0005-S0005
4: 
5: **Feature:** F0005 — One-time document ingestion
6:

8: 
9: **Priority:** High
10: 
11: **Phase:** MVP — v0.1A
12: 
13: **Status:** Not Started
14: 
15: ## User Story
16: 
17: **As a** document intelligence engineer
18: 
19: **I want** every published block to carry an observed text origin and exact OCR provenance before the v2 writer is switched on
20: 
21: **So that** downstream admission and review never rely on inferred origins or unidentified OCR models.
22: 
23: ## Context & Background
24: 
25: F0004 delivered v2 content contracts, per-item origin projection, the `content.v2_writer_enabled` switch (default `false`) and the per-document v1 fallback, and its Step 1a spike passed on three synthetic fixtures. F0004 closeout assigned F0005 two obligations before broader activation: fixed Docling/hook qualification and exact OCR model-asset provenance. This story also satisfies the 2026-09-25 A-4 activation prerequisite under ADR-0065. See the [PRD](PRD.md).
26: 
27: ## Acceptance Criteria
28: 
29: - [ ] **F0005-S0005-AC1:** Given every qualification-corpus document converted live, when per-item origin is projected, then each native text item is `STATED` with its observed producer, each OCR item is `OCR` with engine name/version, and items that cannot be attributed return `ORIGIN_UNAVAILABLE`; no origin is derived from page OCR counts.
30: - [ ] **F0005-S0005-AC2:** Given OCR ran, when the bundle is published, then the OCR model assets used are identified by name, revision and file SHA-256 read from the installed asset files; an unidentifiable asset blocks v2 publication for that document instead of recording a guessed identity.
31: - [ ] **F0005-S0005-AC3:** Given a document with any `ORIGIN_UNAVAILABLE` item and the v2 writer enabled, when it is published, then it falls back to a v1 bundle with `writer_fallback_reason=ORIGIN_UNAVAILABLE` audited, and the per-corpus fallback rate is reported.
32: - [ ] **F0005-S0005-AC4:** Given v2 bundles from the corpus, when every existing consumer reads them (authorized content reader, evidence resolver, Graph adapter saved-JSON path, importer, restore drill), then they pass with unchanged behavior; v1 bundles stay readable.
33: - [ ] **F0005-S0005-AC5:** Given AC1–AC4 pass and S0004 activation is accepted, when `content.v2_writer_enabled` is set to `true` in committed configuration, then new sources publish v2 bundles and existing artifacts are not rewritten.
34: - [ ] **F0005-S0005-AC6:** Given a job, artifact or run state change, when the operation succeeds or fails, then an audit event records actor and job identity, correlation and attempt IDs, artifact/run references, outcome and timestamp, without secrets or source text.
35: 
36: **Edge Cases & Error Scenarios:** OCR assets present but altered after install → digest mismatch blocks v2 publication. Mixed page with an image region OCR skipped → that region's items `ORIGIN_UNAVAILABLE`, document falls back to v1. Docling upgrade → origin hook re-qualified before the switch stays on.
37:

45: 
46: **Validation and audit:** the switch change is recorded with the activation decision; fallbacks are audited per document.
47:

50: Per-item origin and producer, OCR engine name/version, OCR asset name/revision/SHA-256, fallback reason and rate, manifest contract version.

54: Unchanged from F0004: origin metadata follows artifact access.
55: 
56: ## Non-Functional Expectations

58: Origin projection adds no model call and no second conversion.
59: 
60: ## Dependencies

FILE planning-mds/architecture/decisions/ADR-0060-docling-graph-document-pipeline-orchestration.md
1: # ADR-0060: Docling-Graph Document Pipeline Orchestration
2: 
3: ## Status
4: 
5: - [x] Proposed
6: - [ ] Accepted
7: - [ ] Superseded
8: - [ ] Rejected
9: 
10: **Date:** 2026-09-15
11: **Direction authorized by:** Operator; formal ADR acceptance awaits the proof gates below.
12: **Implementation:** Synthetic development proof complete for the current scope. Docling-Graph 1.9.1 is locked; conversion/reuse, PostgreSQL recovery, dense extraction with recorded responses, and multi-region review mapping have bounded proof. Production acceptance and activation remain future work; see [compatibility evidence](../../features/F0005-one-time-docling-ingestion/compatibility-evidence.md). Plan run `2026-10-23-9a04b327` (2026-10-23) defines the acceptance procedure: local live qualification on a synthetic corpus against operator-approved thresholds, independent QA/Architect/Security verdicts and operator acceptance, then activation through committed configuration with a direct-baseline rollback. See the F0005 [PRD](../../features/F0005-one-time-docling-ingestion/PRD.md) and [assembly plan](../../features/F0005-one-time-docling-ingestion/feature-assembly-plan.md).
13: **Would refine:** ADR-0003, ADR-0004, ADR-0028, ADR-0040, ADR-0055, ADR-0058, ADR-0059. Reconsiders the exclusion recorded after F0001; the current runtime decision, accepted content/evidence contracts, and F0001 measurements remain in force pending proof.
14: 
15: ## Context
16: 
17: F0001 planned a Docling-Graph proof but recorded a substitution: Docling performed conversion and a Nebula adapter called vLLM directly for extraction. The [dependency matrix](../../../docker/DEPENDENCY-MATRIX.md) records F0001's unsuccessful reuse attempt and the later correction: the pinned 1.9.1 candidate does support a separate native-JSON input path. The archived [F0001 status](../../features/archive/F0001-repository-and-engineering-foundation/STATUS.md) records that deviation. The file name `docling_graph_adapter.py` does not establish use of the upstream package. That adapter joins the persisted document text into one request; `test_parse_once_reinterpret.py` expects one model call per profile. F0001 did not prove chunked extraction. The switch must measure the change in calls, cost, context behavior, and evidence fidelity.
18: 
19: Upstream's current input guide documents loading DoclingDocument JSON without conversion. This warrants a fresh integration proof; it does not invalidate the recorded F0001 run. Documentation on `main` and an installed release with the same version string need not contain the same code. F0005 must identify the tested release or immutable commit and its dependency lock before enabling the new path.
20: 
21: ## Proposed Decision
22: 
23: Use `docling-project/docling-graph` as the document pipeline coordinator in `neuron/`. Docling remains its conversion engine and the source of the native document representation. The switch replaces Nebula's custom coordination of specialist extraction steps; it does not remove Docling from the dependency graph.
24: 
25: | Owner | Responsibility |
26: |---|---|
27: | Docling-Graph | Coordinate document conversion through Docling, template-driven extraction, chunking, model calls, output validation, and extraction graph/provenance production within a processing attempt |
28: | Nebula ingestion and artifact adapters | Authorize input; identify source/version; publish and reuse the immutable content bundle through `ContentArtifactStore`; map native items to stable evidence references |
29: | Nebula interpretation adapter | Compile released profiles into supported templates/configuration; map output to `InterpretationResult`; enforce context/cost limits and evidence fidelity |
30: | Nebula engine | Persist jobs and runs, authorize access, recover failed attempts, review assertions, resolve enterprise identities, and commit canonical facts with audit/outbox |
31: | Temporal, from F0050 | Durable business workflows, timers, external-system activities, and human waits; invoke the same bounded document operation when needed |
32: 
33: ### Artifact checkpoint and reuse
34: 
35: The ingestion operation coordinates conversion through Docling-Graph and publishes a complete, hashed native DoclingDocument plus normalized bundle before fallible semantic extraction proceeds. A retry after this checkpoint loads the saved native JSON. Profile changes create new interpretation runs over that bundle; they never submit the original PDF or a Markdown export as a substitute for the native JSON reuse path.
36: 
37: F0005 implements and tests a version-pinned checkpoint stage immediately before upstream extraction, with a stage-layout guard. This is a Nebula integration seam, not an upstream durability guarantee. The inspected upstream orchestrator runs extraction before its optional export stages, and API mode does not dump artifacts by default. A single `run_pipeline()` call with an output directory is therefore insufficient evidence of crash-safe parse reuse. Dependency upgrades must revalidate the seam and publication ordering; do not silently relax the invariant or reach into unpinned internals.
38: 
39: There is one accepted artifact per scoped document version and parser configuration. A crash before durable publication may require a conversion retry; after publication, downstream failures must never repeat conversion or OCR. Record attempts separately from accepted artifacts. Nebula owns leases, heartbeats, cancellation, idempotent publication, and job/outbox recovery under section 114.1; upstream in-process stage execution does not supply those guarantees.
40: 
41: ### Extraction, provenance, and authority
42: 
43: - F0015 compiles versioned Nebula profiles to trusted Pydantic templates and explicit pipeline configuration. Templates are application-controlled; user profile fields cannot select arbitrary Python imports. The compiler retains Nebula's ontology annotations, qualifiers, units, and schema hash.
44: - F0016 records the artifact hash, profile/template and schema hashes, pipeline revision/configuration, model/prompt identity, selected blocks, attempt, token usage, and outcome. Extraction graph, provenance ledger, and effective configuration are immutable run outputs, stored separately from the content bundle. Reinterpretation never mutates the parse artifact.
45: - F0005 proves a mapping from upstream chunks and native item references into Nebula block/table locators. A node identifier's text match does not ground every property or relationship. Preserve upstream precision and merge/derived lineage; unresolved or document-level grounding cannot become a fabricated span. ADR-0058 continues to govern evidence resolution and review.
46: - Upstream graph IDs and deduplication are local to extraction. F0017 owns tenant-scoped enterprise identity; F0018 owns canonical commits. Graph exports cannot write directly to canonical tables or the AGE projection. Assertions and relationship candidates enter the existing review and commit path.
47: - Keep the self-hosted vLLM policy from ADR-0055. Enforce input plus schema/prompt plus output token budgets before every model call, including repair, fill, and reconciliation calls. Revalidate the 4,096-token profile; upstream templates do not establish model adequacy. Keep source-bearing exports access-controlled and outside routine logs.
48: 
49: ### Runtime ownership and handoff
50: 
51: The candidate `DoclingGraphPipelineAdapter` in `brain-extraction` owns calls into the upstream package. `brain-ingestion` supplies the conversion checkpoint callback and keeps `build_bundle()` / `ContentArtifactStore` publication. The shared parse-result DTO now lives in a neutral contract rather than the concrete `docling_adapter.py` module; neither package imports the other to construct that DTO. The adapter provides the native document and parse quality (status, failed pages, OCR counts, warnings) to the callback before semantic extraction, through the tested, pinned stage integration. The callback finishes publication before processing continues. Later interpretation enters the same adapter with the persisted native JSON and performs no conversion. `PipelineContext` and upstream export paths remain private adapter details.
52: 
53: F0005's candidate includes the checkpoint seam, package declarations, `neuron/uv.lock`, build/run metadata, and opt-in composition wiring. The direct client is now named `OpenAICompatibleExtractionAdapter` and remains the comparison baseline; its old name is a compatibility import. S0004 has recorded-response direct/dense comparison evidence, while production qualification and activation remain future work. A class rename alone is not migration evidence.
54: 
55: ### What becomes simpler
56: 
57: Nebula delegates chunk/extract/validate/graph coordination to one specialist pipeline and one configured model backend. After acceptance, retire duplicated extraction control flow in the current direct-vLLM adapter. Retain thin adapters for artifact publication, profile compilation, scope enforcement, evidence translation, and the provider-neutral result contract. Do not duplicate these business contracts inside upstream templates.
58: 
59: Docling-Graph does not replace Temporal. v0.1 keeps PostgreSQL-backed durable jobs; F0050 remains v0.3. Temporal should schedule bounded document operations, not reproduce Docling-Graph's internal chunk loop. Both paths call the same authorized application service; neither owns canonical truth.
60: 
61: ## Delivery and acceptance
62: 
63: | Feature | Required change / evidence |
64: |---|---|
65: | F0004 | Retain the native six-file bundle and parser identity; separately record pipeline identity and interpretation-output manifests. Preserve ADR-0059 storage and hash verification. |
66: | F0005 | Pin upstream and transitive dependencies; prove native and scanned conversion, durable checkpoint before model failure, zero conversion/OCR on two later profiles, restart reuse, duplicate-job isolation, and cancellation/retry without duplicate accepted effects. |

69: | F0017 / F0018 | Verify graph-local merging cannot resolve enterprise identity or bypass review, authorization, or commit idempotency. |

72: | F0032 | Prove affected-content-only reinterpretation through F0016, with stable original references and zero conversion; explicitly reject unsupported targeting. |

76: F0005's proof may use two small fixed templates before the production F0015 compiler exists. F0015/F0016 then deliver the production interpretation path against the accepted adapter. Do not create a circular dependency between the foundation proof and the full compiler.
77: 
78: ADR acceptance and activation require observed tests against the real upstream package and local model, not a renamed mock or the existing direct client. Failure injection must cover a crash after conversion publication and a model failure before graph export. Keep the F0001 runtime as the measured baseline until those gates pass; do not claim it validates Docling-Graph. Record the selected package/source digest, measured limitations, and results in the dependency matrix. Archived F0001 requirements, results, and Done status remain historical records.

80: ## Proof gates before acceptance
81: 
82: These are production-acceptance requirements. The current early-development scope uses synthetic fixtures and is complete without claiming these release gates. The [synthetic comparison](../../features/F0005-one-time-docling-ingestion/synthetic-comparison.json) proves dense skeleton/fill orchestration with recorded responses; its estimated tokens and local timing are not model-quality or serving-cost measurements. On its longer fixtures, Graph direct lacks usable item refs and remains unresolved; dense preserves the refs. Multi-region grounding requires complete character coverage and retains separate selectors through review. Unsupported relationship output still fails closed.
83: 
84: - Exact release/commit and digest plus locked dependencies; native and scanned processing, durable parse publication, worker restart, and two saved-JSON profiles with instrumented zero conversion/OCR on reuse.
85: - Ground limit amount, currency, basis, effective date, table headers, and values supported by multiple regions; preserve the provenance ledger and unresolved precision.
86: - Compare the current single-request baseline with Graph chunking on an agreed corpus: schema validity, accuracy, abstention, tokens/calls, latency, and review workload. Cover timeouts, partial conversion, invalid extraction, retries, and worker restart.
87: - Verify local vLLM configuration, secret redaction, bounded worker/model concurrency (including upstream internal fan-out), and tenant access/retention for source-bearing outputs.
88: 
89: **Acceptance scope (2026-10-23).** Under the operator's F0005 scope decision, acceptance on F0005's local qualification governs the local Docker Compose runtime only. Production host qualification and frozen-corpus acceptance remain F0026 obligations, and they may reopen this decision. A rejection keeps the direct client as the runtime default and records the failed gates here.
90: 
91: Evidence and reviewer verdicts belong in [F0005 STATUS](../../features/F0005-one-time-docling-ingestion/STATUS.md). The pin, native/scanned conversion, publication ordering, native-JSON reuse, scalar/table evidence, and durable recovery have bounded test evidence, including PostgreSQL queue and worker/importer process-exit recovery with recorded model responses. The synthetic comparison also proves dense extraction orchestration and multi-region grounding through import and review. Production gates remain open: live comparative extraction and property/multi-region evidence qualification, unsupported relationship semantics, full deployment recovery, operational qualification, and reviewer acceptance. Structural planning checks cannot change this ADR to Accepted.
