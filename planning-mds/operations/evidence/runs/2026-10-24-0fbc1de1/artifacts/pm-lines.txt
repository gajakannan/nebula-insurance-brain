11: ## Feature Statement
12: 
13: **As a** document intelligence engineer
14: **I want** durable one-time ingestion and repeatable interpretation of saved native content, qualified against a live local model
15: **So that** model failures and profile changes never repeat published parsing or lose source evidence, and the Docling-Graph pipeline replaces the direct client only on measured evidence.
16: 
17: ## Business Objective
18: 
19: Decide ADR-0060 on observed local evidence. The candidate pipeline (Docling-Graph 1.9.1 over Docling 2.126.0) is implemented and opt-in, with synthetic, recorded-response proof only. F0005 finishes when the pipeline is either qualified and activated as the local default, or rejected with the F0001 direct client retained. Both outcomes are complete if recorded with evidence.
20: 
21: ## Problem Statement
22: 
23: The current proof never ran a live model through Graph, never measured quality or cost against the direct baseline, and never injected live-provider or storage failures. Since it was written, F0002–F0004 shipped verified principals, persistence discipline, versioned content contracts, text-origin records and a disabled v2 writer. F0004 handed F0005 two obligations: exact OCR model-asset provenance and fixed Docling/hook qualification before broader v2 activation. Without this plan, the candidate stays opt-in indefinitely and downstream features (F0014–F0016, F0024) plan against an unqualified extraction path.

81: ## Data Requirements
82: 
83: Keep separate source, content-artifact and interpretation identities (ADR-0004). Conversion identity: parser name/version/configuration, OCR engine name/version and exact OCR model-asset identity (name, revision, file digest). Interpretation identity: Graph revision, extraction contract (direct or dense), template/schema hash, chunking settings, serving model id and revision, tokenizer revision. Graph chunks, extraction graph and provenance ledger are run-owned outputs, never bundle content.

85: **Qualification corpus:** at least 12 synthetic GL declarations documents outside the frozen holdout — at least 6 native, 4 scanned and 2 mixed-page — covering unique values, repeated values, table-borne limits, values split across regions, absent fields and one partial conversion. Expected values and evidence locations for all five profile fields are authored from the document source, before and independently of any extraction output, and committed with their hashes.
86: 
87: **Validation rules:** missing values stay missing; unknown origin stays `ORIGIN_UNAVAILABLE` and triggers v1 fallback; a node-identifier match cannot ground all of its properties; unsupported targeting or relationship output is an explicit failure.

128: ## Step 7 — Comparison, review and decision (S0004, S0005-AC5)
129: 
130: **Harness:** `neuron/benchmarks/f0005_qualification.py --corpus neuron/fixtures/qualification --pipelines direct-baseline graph-dense graph-direct --output <run>/artifacts/qualification/report.json`. It reuses the scoring of `docling_graph_comparison.py`, runs through the worker composition against live vLLM, and records configuration hashes, pins, corpus and expectation hashes, and per-case rows. Graph direct is reported but is not a candidate.
131: 
132: **Metrics,** exactly as the PRD defines them:
133: 
134: - Accuracy: normalized exact match. Normalization is limited to whitespace collapse and Unicode NFC; no currency or date reformatting.
135: - Abstention: computed over expected-null fields.
136: - Fabricated evidence: resolved selectors whose re-read text fails the value comparison.
137: - Evidence resolution: exact span or cell over correct values.
138: - Latency: wall time from interpretation start to result publication per document-profile; p95 by nearest rank.
139: - Review workload: review items created per document.
140: 
141: **Void runs:** if the provider fails mid-measurement, the whole report is void and rerun. Reports are immutable; reruns get a new file.
142: 
143: **Decision sequence:**
144: 
145: 1. Mark each threshold met or missed in `report-summary.md`.
146: 2. The three independent review sessions write verdicts with evidence references into STATUS's append-only provenance table.
147: 3. The operator records acceptance or rejection.
148: 4. **On acceptance:** in one change, set `extraction.pipeline: graph-dense` and `content.v2_writer_enabled: true`; mark ADR-0060 Accepted with the evidence links; update `docker/DEPENDENCY-MATRIX.md`, build/run metadata, the C4 container description and BLUEPRINT §2.1/§4.8. Then submit one new corpus document and verify the pipeline, run identity and v2 manifest after a restart. Rollback is verified by switching `extraction.pipeline: direct-baseline`, submitting again, and confirming earlier artifacts and runs are unchanged.
149: 5. **On rejection:** both settings stay as they are. ADR-0060 records the failed gates and stays Proposed, or becomes Rejected if the operator says so. The direct-baseline worker path remains available as built.
